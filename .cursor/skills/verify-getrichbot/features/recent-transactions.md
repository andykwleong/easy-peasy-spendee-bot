# Transactions

Transactions shows the confirmed rows for the month you pick, opens on the current month, can open any other month that has a saved row, can narrow to one person, can filter the rows already on the page, and can correct a row's category from the same list.

## Sub-features

- `recent-locked` shows the locked page before Telegram confirms the visitor.
- `recent-both` shows both people's September rows after Both is chosen. Sample archive stays hidden because it is August 2025.
- `recent-month` opens August 2026, which has no rows, then August 2025, which has Sample archive, then returns to September 2026.
- `recent-alex` shows only rows logged by Alex.
- `recent-sam` shows only rows logged by Sam.
- `recent-dots` shows `Message sent` on Sample cafe and `message fail` on Sample market.
- `recent-fix` opens Fix tagging and saves a new category immediately.
- `recent-search` filters the loaded rows by shop, category, or amount. An empty box shows the loaded list.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the dashboard web page. Before login the page is locked. After login the first screen is Transactions.
- In Telegram, send `/dashboard` in a private chat or the household group. The bot replies with a button named `Open dashboard`. This harness does not open that chat.
- On Transactions, choose `Andy`, `FX`, or `Both`. The page opens on the signed-in person. Alex opens on Andy. Sam opens on FX. Andy is Alex's rows. FX is Sam's rows. Those four chips, plus `No category`, stay on the right of the title.
- Under the title `Transactions`, the month dropdown is named `Month`, the year dropdown is named `Year`, and the search box is named `Search transactions`. The placeholder says `Search shop, category, or amount`. September and 2026 start selected. August and 2025 are also available because Sample archive is 14 Aug 2025.
- On a row, choose `Fix tagging`.

## Gotchas

- The chips are `Andy`, `FX`, `Both`, and `No category`, in that order, for either signed-in person. `Andy` asks for scope `Alex`. `FX` asks for scope `Sam`. Alex opens on Andy. Sam opens on FX. Sample cafe shows `Logged by Andy` and `Message sent`. Sample market shows `Logged by FX` and `message fail`. The sheet names Alex and Sam stay off the row. A row with no group-note mark shows neither word. This seed has a mark on both rows.
- A search box sits under the title, after the month and year dropdowns. Its accessible name is `Search transactions`. These steps leave it empty. Typing filters shop, category, and amount on the rows already loaded for the month on screen. It does not request the sheet again. Choosing a month or a year does not request the sheet again either. The month and year stay until logout or reload, including after another screen. Cards, Month, and Eval do not show the box.
- The row's delete control is named `Delete Sample cafe`, not `Delete`. `Delete` is the confirm button inside the dialog.
- Sample Visa has no channel other than All, so Fix tagging does not show a channel chooser. Do not invent one.
- Choosing a category saves immediately. The open row's Save label closes the panel, and it saves an amount only when that amount changed. X closes without saving. After this recipe, Month shows Groceries instead of Food until a fresh launch.
- A save line by itself is not proof. `sheet.json` has to show `Groceries` as well.
- Pressing the verification against the Railway URL, or starting the bot, invalidates the run.
